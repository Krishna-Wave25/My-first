Why This System

From Energy Loss to Intelligent Control

⚠ The Problem

Traditional systems waste solar energy

Solar panel output constantly changes with sunlight, temperature, battery condition and load demand. Without intelligent control:

  • ✕ Energy is wasted when panels run off their maximum power point
  • ✕ Battery life is reduced by poor charging and deep discharge
  • ✕ Faults go undetected for long periods, damaging hardware
  • ✕ No visibility into generation, load or system efficiency
✓ The Solution

An intelligent, connected microgrid

MPPT extracts maximum solar power while IoT technology monitors every parameter in real time and uploads it to the cloud:

  • ✓ MPPT tracking maximizes power extraction under all conditions
  • ✓ Live monitoring of voltage, current, power & temperature
  • ✓ Cloud database stores data for analytics and reporting
  • ✓ Dashboard detects faults and manages loads remotely
