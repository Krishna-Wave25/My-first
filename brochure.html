IoT-Based Solar Microgrid Energy Management System with MPPT

Maximum Power Point Tracking · IoT Telemetry · Cloud Analytics · AI Forecasting

Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering · Final Year Project · 2025 – 2026
Document: SolarGrid_MPPT_Brochure · Version 1.0 · Pages 5

Abstract

This project presents an IoT-Based Solar Microgrid Energy Management System with Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) designed to maximize solar power extraction and improve energy management. The system integrates a photovoltaic array, an MPPT charge controller, lithium battery storage and an IoT monitoring layer built around an ESP32 controller. Real-time measurements of voltage, current, power, battery state-of-charge, temperature and load consumption are streamed over MQTT to a cloud database and rendered on a live web dashboard. The platform delivers MPPT optimization, fault detection, remote load control, energy analytics and AI-based generation and load forecasting, improving efficiency, reliability and sustainability compared with conventional fixed-voltage solar systems.

Index Terms — IoT, Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT), Solar Microgrid, ESP32, MQTT, Battery Energy Storage, Cloud Analytics, AI Forecasting.

I. Introduction

Traditional solar energy systems frequently lose efficiency because the output of a solar panel changes continuously with sunlight, temperature, battery condition and load demand. Without intelligent monitoring and control, energy is wasted, battery life is reduced and faults may go undetected for long periods. This project replaces the passive approach with an active, connected microgrid: MPPT algorithms extract the maximum possible power from the array, while IoT sensors give operators complete real-time visibility and remote control over the entire energy flow — from panel to load.

II. Problem Statement

Conventional solar installations operate at a fixed voltage point that rarely matches the panel's true Maximum Power Point, particularly under partial shading or changing irradiance. Operators lack real-time insight into generation, storage, consumption and thermal state, delaying fault response and preventing optimal scheduling. The objective of this project is to solve these problems by combining MPPT control, IoT sensing, cloud storage and a web dashboard into a single intelligent energy management system.

III. System Architecture

The system is organized into two complementary layers: a power layer that converts and stores solar energy, and an IoT telemetry layer that senses, transmits and visualizes data.

Power path: Solar Panel (320W × 4) → MPPT Controller (P&O / IncCond / AI) → Battery Bank (48V LiFePO₄) → Inverter (1.5 kVA) → AC / DC Loads.

Telemetry path: IoT Sensors (V / I / Temp) → ESP32 (MQTT · Wi-Fi) → Cloud Database (Time-series) → Web Dashboard (Live analytics), with control commands returned over the same channel.

IV. MPPT Technology

Maximum Power Point Tracking continuously adjusts the operating voltage and current of the array so the maximum possible power is extracted under changing sunlight and temperature. The controller compares power before and after each perturbation and converges on the peak of the P-V curve, where dP/dV = 0. The implemented algorithms are:

  • Perturb & Observe — classic hill-climbing; simple and robust, ideal for stable irradiance.
  • Incremental Conductance — compares I/V with dI/dV for precise tracking under rapid changes.
  • Fuzzy Logic MPPT — rule-based controller that handles non-linear and partial-shading cases.
  • AI-Based MPPT — machine-learning model predicts the optimum point ahead of irradiance shifts.

V. Dashboard & System Features

  • Real-time monitoring of solar voltage, current, power, battery percentage, load, temperature.
  • Animated energy-flow visualization between panel → MPPT → battery → inverter → load.
  • Live sensor telemetry charts for voltage, current, power, battery SOC, temperature and efficiency.
  • Fault detection with severity classification, alert feed and full incident history.
  • Remote load control via a 4-channel relay module with smart scheduling.
  • AI weather-based prediction of solar generation and load demand for the next 48 hours.
  • CO₂-savings and energy-savings reporting with exportable monthly summaries.

VII. Applications & Benefits

Applications: rural electrification, smart homes, agriculture and irrigation, telecom towers, small industries, emergency backup, EV charging stations and smart city infrastructure.

Benefits: maximum solar extraction, reduced wastage, better battery management, real-time control, lower electricity cost, improved reliability and measurable carbon reduction.

VIII. Future Scope

  • AI-based energy optimization and reinforcement-learning controllers.
  • Native mobile app with push notifications and voice-assistant support.
  • EV charging integration with vehicle-to-grid (V2G) capability.
  • Blockchain-based peer-to-peer energy trading between microgrid neighbours.
  • Grid-connected operation with net metering and demand-response services.
  • Advanced fault prediction using vibration, thermal and electrical signatures.
  • Large-scale smart city deployment with centralized energy intelligence.

IX. Conclusion

The IoT-Based Solar Microgrid Energy Management System with MPPT demonstrates how the combination of power electronics, IoT, cloud computing and machine learning can transform a conventional solar installation into an intelligent, self-optimizing energy platform. With 98%+ MPPT tracking efficiency, real-time telemetry, automated fault response and AI-based forecasting, the system delivers measurable gains in energy yield, reliability and sustainability — providing a scalable blueprint for smart microgrids of the future.
