Distinctive thread: "agency ledger" — a well-kept accounts book for a creative agency. Warm paper ground, deep ink text, hairline rules instead of heavy boxes, tabular numerals everywhere money appears, and a single terracotta signal color reserved for what needs attention (key figures, active stage, primary action).
Modes: both light (default, paper) and dark (ink-black desk with warm off-white text). Accent stays terracotta in both.
Pacing: tight. This is a working dashboard — compact rows, dense tables, small caps labels, generous only around the headline revenue figure.
Type: Fraunces (display, optical sizing) for headline numbers and section titles; Inter Tight for UI and body; JetBrains Mono for small meta (dates, IDs). All figures use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.
Surfaces: cards are a slightly deeper paper tone than the ground with a 1px hairline border and almost no shadow; radius small (6–10px). Charts use the terracotta accent as primary series, muted inks for comparison series.
Status (deal stages, lead sources) is differentiated by position, small dot markers and weight — not a rainbow of saturated hues. Success/warning/error only for genuine state.
