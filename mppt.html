The Core Technology

Finding the Sweet Spot of Every Panel

A solar panel’s power output depends on voltage, irradiance and temperature. The P-V curve has a single peak — the Maximum Power Point (MPP). MPPT algorithms hunt for that peak and hold the panel there, even as conditions shift.

The animation on the right shows an MPPT controller driving the operating point to the peak of the power curve, then oscillating tightly around it as it responds to changing irradiance.

MPPT Algorithms

Four Strategies, One Goal

From proven classics to AI-driven prediction — each algorithm keeps the panel at its peak power point.

ALGO 01
⇅

Perturb & Observe

Perturbs the operating voltage in small steps and observes the resulting power change. If power rises, keep going; if it falls, reverse. Simple, robust and the industry standard.

Tracking ~94%
ALGO 02
∑

Incremental Conductance

Compares instantaneous conductance (I/V) with incremental conductance (dI/dV) to locate the exact MPP. More precise and stable in rapidly changing irradiance.

Tracking ~97%
ALGO 03
◎

Fuzzy Logic MPPT

Uses fuzzy rules on error and change-of-error to adapt the operating point. Handles non-linearities and partial shading conditions with smooth, fast convergence.

Tracking ~97.5%
ALGO 04
⚡

AI-Based MPPT

Machine-learning models learn the panel’s behaviour over time and predict the optimum point ahead of irradiance changes. Best dynamic response and highest yield.

Tracking ~98.5%
