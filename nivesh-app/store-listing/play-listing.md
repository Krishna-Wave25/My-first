## Full description
Nivesh helps beginners understand the stock market before risking real money.

• Market overview – Nifty 50, Sensex, Bank Nifty, top gainers and losers, sector trends (delayed data)
• Stock pages – price charts for 1D to 5Y, day and 52-week range, watchlist
• Practice mode – buy and sell with ₹1,00,000 of virtual money and see how you would have done
• SIP calculator – see how compounding works at the return rate you choose
• Investing basics – learn what kinds of options exist for small and large amounts, with risk labels (education, not advice)
• AI explainer – ask "What is a SIP?" or "What does P/E mean?" and get simple answers
• Screener – filter stocks by sector and daily move

Try everything free for 7 days. Then subscribe in the app (cancel anytime in Google Play).

Important: Nivesh is for education only. It does not place real trades, is not a broker, and does not give investment advice. Prices are delayed and may be inaccurate. Investments are subject to market risk; returns are never guaranteed.
