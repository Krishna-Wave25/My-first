## What the app does
Nivesh is an investing-education and market-tracking app. It is not a broker, does not hold your money, and does not give personalised investment advice.

## Data we handle

| Data | Where it lives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Watchlist, practice (virtual) portfolio, practice orders | **Only on your phone** | So the app can show them to you. We never receive this. |
| Free-trial start date, subscription status | On your phone | To decide whether to show the paywall. |
| Questions you type into the AI explainer (and the last few messages of that chat) | Sent to our server, which forwards them to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce an answer. We do not link them to your name, and we do not intentionally store them. | To answer your question. Do not type personal or financial account details. |
| Google Play purchase token | Sent to our server and to Google Play | To confirm your subscription is real and active. |
| Your IP address | Seen by our server (for abuse limits) and by market-data providers when your phone fetches prices | Technical necessity and rate limiting. |

We do **not** require an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, location, photos, or advertising ID. We do not sell your data and show no ads.
